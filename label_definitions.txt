evidence: makes a point backed by something a stranger could check without trusting the writer, such as a link, a quote, a number, a date, or a command
opinion: a judgment, prediction, or argument with no checkable support offered, including rhetorical questions
experience: a first-person account of what the writer did, used, or saw, where the only support is that the writer says so
question: a genuine request for information, an explanation, or a recommendation that someone could answer
